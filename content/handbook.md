# Al Noor Contracting, worker handbook (extract)

This is seed content for the demo. It is a short, plain-language extract of the
kind of handbook a UAE contractor gives its workers, and it is the first place
Jisr looks when a worker asks a general question. Jisr quotes it, names it as the
source, and never turns it into legal advice.

## Working hours

Normal working time is 8 hours a day, 48 hours a week. Anything beyond that is
overtime and must be recorded on your timesheet and signed by your supervisor.

Overtime is paid at 1.25x your normal hourly rate. Overtime worked between
10 PM and 4 AM is paid at 1.5x. If you think overtime is missing from your pay,
send Jisr a voice note with the month and the number of hours, and a photo of
your timesheet if you have one.

## The midday break

From **15 June to 15 September**, work in direct sun or in an open-air workplace
is not allowed between **12:30 PM and 3:00 PM**.

During that break your employer provides shade, drinking water and a rest area.
If you are asked to work outdoors during the midday break, tell Jisr. You can do
that anonymously by saying "anonymous". Your name is not shown to anyone.

## Rest days and leave

- One rest day a week, normally Friday. If you work on your rest day you get
  either a replacement day off or overtime pay.
- Annual leave is 30 calendar days a year once you have completed one year of
  service. Between six months and a year, it is 2 days a month.
- Sick leave: tell your supervisor on the first day and send a medical
  certificate. The first 15 days are on full pay.
- Public holidays are announced in advance. Jisr sends them to you as a voice
  note in your own language.

## Pay

- Salary is paid monthly through the Wage Protection System (WPS).
- Your payslip shows basic pay, allowances, overtime and deductions.
- Deductions have to be explained to you in writing before they are made.
- If your salary is late or wrong, report it. A pay correction needs two
  different people to approve it, so it is checked before anything changes.

## Accommodation and transport

- Rooms are cleaned weekly; air conditioning faults are a maintenance priority.
- Report a broken AC, water problem or pest issue by scanning the sticker on
  your room door and speaking.
- Company buses run to a published timetable. Changes are broadcast through
  Jisr as a voice note.

## Safety

- Wear your PPE. If it is damaged or missing, ask for a replacement. You will
  not be charged for it.
- Stop work if you believe a task is unsafe, and tell your supervisor.
- **In an emergency, call 998 (ambulance), 997 (fire and civil defence) or
  999 (police) first.** Then tell Jisr, and the safety officer is notified
  immediately.

## Who to contact

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| Room, AC, water, pests | Camp boss, or scan the sticker on your door |
| Timesheets and overtime | Your site supervisor |
| Salary, WPS, payslips | HR |
| Passport, visa, Emirates ID, labour card | HR |
| Anything you do not want to put your name to | Say "anonymous" to Jisr |

For a complaint your employer cannot resolve, the UAE Ministry of Human
Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) is the official channel. Jisr will point you
to MOHRE's own published contact details rather than giving you legal advice.
